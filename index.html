How It Works

A straightforward service experience

From your first call to the completed repair, we keep the process simple, transparent, and focused on getting your equipment back to work.

Request Service

Contact us by phone or submit a service request online. Provide your vehicle or equipment details and tell us what issues you're experiencing.

Inspect & Diagnose

We perform a thorough inspection and diagnostic evaluation to identify the root cause of the problem, whether mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, or drivetrain-related.

Review Your Estimate

Once the issue is identified, we explain the recommended repairs and provide an estimate for your approval before work begins.

Repair & Get Back to Work

We complete the approved repairs and verify the work performed, helping get your vehicle or equipment back on the road or job site.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions before you call.

Do you offer mobile diesel repair?

Yes. We provide mobile and roadside repair services for trucks and heavy equipment throughout our service area, subject to availability and job requirements.

Do you work on heavy equipment?

Yes. We service various types of construction and agricultural equipment, including excavators, loaders, forklifts, and tractors.

Do you service commercial fleets?

Yes. We provide maintenance, diagnostics, and repair services for commercial vehicles and fleet customers.

Can I bring my vehicle to your shop?

Yes. In-shop service is available for qualifying vehicles and equipment. Contact us to discuss your equipment and service needs.

Do you offer preventive maintenance?

Yes. We provide routine maintenance services to help keep trucks and equipment operating reliably.

Do you install customer-supplied parts?

Yes. Customer-supplied parts may be installed depending on the application and job requirements.

How do I request a repair?

Call or text 215-645-2146, or submit a service request through our website.

What areas do you service?

We serve Philadelphia and surrounding areas. Contact us with your location to confirm service availability.
